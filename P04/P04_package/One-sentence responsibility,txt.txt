One-sentence responsibility for each module
Module Responsibilities – Practical 04

__init__.py – Initializes the delivery package and exposes its main functions.

data.py – Loads the delivery dataset and splits it into training and testing data.

features.py – Contains feature-related functions used for delivery prediction.

model.py – Trains, evaluates, saves, and loads the delivery prediction model.

validate.py – Checks whether a delivery order contains valid values.

train.py – Trains the delivery prediction model and saves it to disk.

predict.py – Loads the saved model and predicts delivery time for a new order.
